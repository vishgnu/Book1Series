# m03: The Honest Pound

Revision log. Genre: Cozy fantasy mystery. Target readers: Women 25–45.

## Where it started

**Seed idea** (written by the series developer before any drafting):

> Working title: The Honest Pound. In the canal city of Brindlemarket magic is a trade like any other, sold by weight and measure: luck by the ounce, bottled weather by the gill, sleep by the dram. Magic can make anything look, feel, smell and taste like more than it is. The one thing it cannot touch is the dull bronze of the city's master weights, cast before the first charm was sold, so the scale is the only place in Brindlemarket where a glamour fails. The Office of Weights and Measures keeps the masters, seals every scale and measure in the city, and is cordially disliked by everyone who sells anything. Lead: Wren Calloway, 34, Junior Inspector Third Class, exact, shy, fond of marzipan; daughter of a spice merchant ruined for a false scale, a verdict she has never believed. Every case starts with something that weighs wrong (short-weight saffron at the docks, a sleeping draught three drams light, a granary that balances on paper and not on the beam) and ends with a murder solved through quantities, seals, ledgers and residues. Cast ideas: her kind, idle senior inspector; a canal bargewoman who carries what the city would rather not weigh; her landlady of the Bakers' Guild; a rival from the Assay Hall. Thread: someone is forging the Office's own seals, and her father's ruin is tied to it. Titles from measure idioms (Short Weight, Baker's Dozen, False Bottom, Full Measure). An honest clerk, a set of weights and a city that would rather not be measured.

**Originality audit of the seed** (web searches, 15 November 2026):

> Closest found: Eliza Quill, Murder, Magic & a Midnight Market (a cozy fantasy mystery at a night market, with a Guild Inspector as victim); Seanan McGuire, In an Absent Dream (a market whose magic enforces fair value); Joseph Roth, Weights and Measures (an inspector lead, literary, no fantasy). No inspector-sleuth or sealed-scale device found. The seed has already shrunk the lantern night market and dropped a magic-enforced fair bargain.
