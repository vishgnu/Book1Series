# Originality audit of the seed ideas

Before any pitch was drafted, each seed idea was checked against published fiction by web search on 15 November 2026. Audit agents ran three or four differently worded searches on each premise (setting, the lead's work, the central device) and one or two on each working title, reading result pages only. Each seed got a verdict:

- **Clear:** nothing close was found.
- **Adjacent:** existing works share some pieces.
- **Collision:** an existing work has the same premise, setting and signature device.

A web search is not a catalog or trademark check. "Free" means no novel with that title turned up on the open web.

## What the audit changed

No seed was a full collision. Five were close enough to an existing series that the concept was replaced, one was rebuilt because it sat too near another seed in the slate, and most of the others were moved or renamed. The replacements were audited in turn, and two of them were changed again.

## Findings by pitch

### m01: piano tuner who hears a piano's last music
- Brian Harvey, the Frank Ryan mysteries (*Beethoven's Tenth*, *Tokyo Girl*): a piano-tuner sleuth, no paranormal element. <https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/brian-harvey/beethovens-tenth/print/>
- Ann Yost, the Bait & Stitch series: Finnish-American cozies on Michigan's Keweenaw peninsula, the same setting niche. <https://www.fictiondb.com/title/a-pattern-for-murder~ann-yost~2283498.htm>
- Joe Cosentino, the Player Piano Mysteries: a player-piano ghost helps solve murders. <https://www.romance.io/books/5f6067077ae0980e02984a3f/the-player-joe-cosentino>
- The piano-echo device was not found. Working title free.

### m02: first idea, a mother-in-law's ghost and her teaspoons (replaced)

- The spoon network itself was not found, but the central relationship was taken, so the concept was dropped.

## Overlaps inside the slate

The auditors also flagged seeds that sat too close to each other. Each was dealt with before drafting:

- **m01 and m06** both turned on a woman's trained ear, an organist and a vanished mentor. m06 lost its organist and both got new running threads.
- **s01, s08 and m07** all sealed a veteran custodian in with strangers. The space-elevator concept was replaced, which left two.
- **s04 and s05** shared the shape of an official visiting a place where the record is wrong. s04 was rebuilt twice.
- **m02 and m08** both had a sleuth working from court papers, a digital-minded nephew, a sheriff's detective and legal wording in the titles. m02's cast and titles were changed.
- **Several running threads** repeated one pattern, someone rigging a system for gain. The threads of m07, s03, s05 and s09 were changed to different kinds of question.
