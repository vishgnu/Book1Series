# Series pitches: twenty one-pagers

Twenty original series concepts, ten mystery and ten science fiction, each written as a one-page pitch and put through rounds of critique and rewriting by test-reader critics. They are candidates for the four-book series planned in `marketresearch/Business plan - four-book series.md`. Nothing here is decided: choosing one, or none, is the author's call.

**Status:** after round 7 of 10 (Market).

## What is in this folder

- **One file per pitch.** The name carries the genre and the target readers; the series title is added when the rounds are done.
- **`logs/`** has one revision log per pitch: the seed idea, every critic's verdict and scores, what each rewrite changed and declined, and what the gate decided.
- **`logs/seed-audit.md`** records the originality audit of the seed ideas and which concepts were replaced.
- **`PROCESS.md`** describes the improvement method and its limits.
- **Git history** has one commit per round, so `git log -p` on a pitch file shows it changing.
