# Series pitches: twenty one-pagers

Twenty original series concepts, ten mystery and ten science fiction, each written as a one-page pitch and put through rounds of critique and rewriting by test-reader critics. They are candidates for the four-book series planned in `marketresearch/Business plan - four-book series.md`. Nothing here is decided: choosing one, or none, is the author's call.

**Status:** complete. All 10 rounds have run, followed by a blind final check, a title check and a slate ranking.

## What is in this folder

- **One file per pitch.** The name carries the genre and the target readers, then the series title.
- **`logs/`** has one revision log per pitch: the seed idea, every critic's verdict and scores, what each rewrite changed and declined, and what the gate decided.
- **`logs/seed-audit.md`** records the originality audit of the seed ideas and which concepts were replaced.
- **`PROCESS.md`** describes the improvement method and its limits.
- **Git history** has one commit per round, so `git log -p` on a pitch file shows it changing.

## How to read the table

- **Rewrites accepted.** Each round produced one rewrite. A blind judge then compared it with the version before it, and the rewrite was kept only if it won. A low count means the pitch was hard to improve, not that it is weak.
- **Panel score.** After the last round, three seats (a target reader, an acquiring editor and a market positioner) scored the first draft and the final version side by side without knowing which was which, on seven criteria from 1 to 10. The table shows the mean.
- **Final vs first draft.** How many of those three seats preferred the final version.
- **Title check.** A web search for existing books with the same series title or book titles: clear, minor (same title exists in another genre or is obscure) or major (a same-genre or well-known book has it). "Then retitled" means the colliding title was replaced by hand afterwards and the new one searched once. It is a search, not a catalog or trademark check.
- **Slate position.** Three judges ranked all the final pitches, one each for reader pull, production fit and distinctiveness. The position combines the three rankings.

All critics, judges and readers are AI personas built from the audience research in `marketresearch/`. They are a structured second opinion, not a market test: none of these numbers predicts sales.

- **reader-pull:** The top three sit in proven high-borrow subscription categories (paranormal cozy, cozy fantasy, culinary cozy) with instantly legible hooks, a warm recurring cast and a personal thread paid off in book four. The rest either target smaller shelves, need explaining, or offer cooler leads and lower emotional stakes.
- **production-fit:** The top three are invented or near-invented settings with no external facts to verify, a small fixed cast, a repeatable per-book template and puzzles simple enough for one editor to check by hand. Everything lower adds real-world technical, legal or historical accuracy, heavy numeric continuity, or tight fair-play logic that a part-time editor would struggle to police.
- **distinctiveness:** The top three pair a premise no neighbouring title shares with an engine that refills itself: century-old cold cases among ageless amnesiacs, an ocean of crewless ships, and a tuner whose gift erases its own evidence with seven pianos already waiting. Lower entries either sit on crowded shelves or spend their engine by book four.

## What these pitches rest on

- **The business plan's assumptions.** Four books of 60,000 to 70,000 words, one complete case or mission per book, written by an AI model from the author's series bible and edited by the author, for subscription readers in the US market.
- **Target readers are hypotheses.** They come from genre-wide survey data in `marketresearch/Audience targets - cozy paranormal mystery.md`, not from data on these subgenres.
- **Facts inside the pitches are unchecked.** Professions, places, law, science and history in the pitches were written by a model and need verifying before any of them is developed.
- **Originality was checked by search, not guaranteed.** See `logs/seed-audit.md` and the title checks in each log.
