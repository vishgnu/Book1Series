# Ballast

**Genre:** Science fiction adventure (racing) · **Readers:** Men and women 30–65 · **Series:** four books, 60,000–70,000 words each, one complete race per book

**Logline.** In the 2190s, wind-reader Anselm Okoro flies an engineless balloon for a miners' cooperative in four races on four worlds, exposing one cheat per race while hunting the truth about the envelope failure that killed his brother.

**The setting.** Band racing is the great sport of the 2190s: three-person crews in gondolas under envelopes of heated gas, riding the jet streams of Venus, Titan, Jupiter and Saturn. No engines, no steering. A crew can only climb or sink to find a wind going its way, and every move costs ballast or burner fuel. A boat that spends both sinks into the dark.

**The lead.** Anselm Okoro, 48, was band racing's best wind-reader until the Great Band killed his brother and co-pilot Tobias six years ago. He has routed ore barges for the Pallas Cooperative since. He wants the inquiry reopened; the price is flying the Marigold under a secondhand envelope of the same make. He talks like a forecast: short, numbered, never louder than the burner.

**The cast.** Dita Halloran, 19, burner prodigy, who meters heat by the gram; Hesper Lindqvist, 60, rigger, who patches seams by feel; Priya Raman, 34, the ore buyer's auditor nobody wants at the launch field; Lucan Dray, 44, lead pilot for the Corvane works team, who flew against Tobias.

**The series engine.** Every book is one race, launch to finish: a route the reader can hold in mind, weather that breaks the plan, a failure repaired with what is aboard, and one act of cheating exposed before the finish. The federation runs four races every season, and the cooperative needs each purse to enter the next.

**The four-book arc.**
1. *Acid Lap* — Venus Cloud Classic, four days in the acid layer: Marigold places third and exposes a works boat's hidden ballast tank.
2. *Cold Fuel* — Titan Endurance, eleven days in orange haze: Dita overrules Anselm, a doctored fuel load is traced, and Marigold wins.
3. *Storm Rim* — Jupiter's Red Spot Rim: a seam splits where Tobias's did, Hesper's patch holds, and a falsified wind feed is exposed.
4. *The Great Band* — Nine days around Saturn's equator: the championship is settled on the last day, and the split seam goes before the federation.

*Running thread:* Why Tobias's envelope failed, and who had it signed off as pilot error; book 4 answers with a fabric lot number and a name.

**Why readers come back.** Each book is a whole race with a finish line and a result, so a reader can stop satisfied or start the next that night. The crew is good at its work, the danger is weather and physics, and the standings carry forward.

**What makes it different.** A space sport with no engines, no weapons and no steering. Tactics come down to two gauges, ballast and heat, and the underdog entry is paid for with ore tithes and bake sales.
