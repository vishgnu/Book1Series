# The Alpine Hut Mysteries

**Genre:** Traditional mystery (Alpine setting) · **Readers:** Women and men 45–70 · **Series:** four books, 60,000–70,000 words each, one complete case per book

**Logline.** At a Swiss hut 2,740 meters up, warden Ursi Feuz, 52, must find the killer among the hut book's names before police can climb up, while feeding and bunking every guest, the killer included.

**The setting.** The Schwarzhorn Hut, Bernese Oberland, is a staffed mountain refuge: 34 bunks, one stove, one radio. Guests walk up four hours and sign the hut book, which never leaves the hut. When weather shuts path and helicopter, police are a day away.

**The lead.** Twenty years on the rescue winch ended with Ursi's knee. The Aarwald Mountain Club gave her one trial year: a fatal accident ends it, a murder does not. Because she needs murder, she makes herself log the accident evidence first. In 2019 she trusted another's count of five on a ledge; the sixth died overnight. Now she counts herself, at dark. "Guests lie about fitness and boots."

**The cast.** Res von Allmen, supply pilot, brings her the crossword; he flew in 2019 and has never said whose count it was. Nora Gilgen, 23, hut assistant, quit medical school and argues about everything but evidence. Wachtmeister Beat Zumbrunn needs her log, or the prosecutor rules accident. Vreni Morf, 84, Emil's sister, makes cheese on the alp below.

**The series engine.** Every book seals the hut with one body and at most eleven guests. Ursi proves murder from boot rack, bed plan and radio log. Suspects are the book's new signers. She can hold no one, only log. Cases keep arriving because the 1961 page records a private agreement over Vreni's alp.

**The four-book arc.**
1. *Death on the August Page* — August storm: a notary who came to read the 1961 page lies below the terrace, boots dry on the rack.
2. *The Winter Room* — October closing party: the club treasurer dies in the winter room, its flue capped a day early, apparently over club money.
3. *Level Four* — March, avalanche level four: guide Lukas Morf, Vreni's great-nephew, lies buried, transceiver off, though Ursi's door check logged it sending; police post an officer.
4. *The Last Signature* — June fog before the club votes on Ursi: an archivist sent to copy the 1961 page dies on the cellar stairs, and the volume vanishes.

*Running thread:* In 1961 a rope of three came back as two, without Emil Morf, yet "E. Morf" is signed every August since, in the same hand, shakier each decade. Book 4 names the signer, shows whose 2019 count failed, and settles whether Vreni keeps her alp and Ursi the hut.

**Why readers come back.** A complete puzzle per book, solvable from the page; no cliffhanger, no gore. Good company: Res's crossword, Nora's arguments, rösti on the stove, a capable woman at work. Each case brings Vreni one fact nearer to her brother.

**What makes it different.** The suspect list is an object: the hut book page. The detective is the host: she cooks breakfast for the person she is about to name.
