# The Hut Warden Mysteries

**Genre:** Traditional mystery (Alpine setting) · **Readers:** Women and men 45–70 · **Series:** four books, 60,000–70,000 words each, one complete case per book

**Logline.** Ursi Feuz, 52, former mountain-rescue paramedic, runs a 34-bed Swiss alpine hut four hours' walk above the last road. When weather seals it and a guest dies, she must find the killer among the names in the hut book.

**The setting.** The Schwarzhorn Hut stands at 2,740 meters in the Bernese Oberland: red-and-white shutters, 34 bunks, one stove, one radio. Supplies arrive by helicopter; guests walk four hours from the last postbus stop and sign the hut book. When storm or snow closes the path, the cantonal police are a day away, and by custom the warden answers for everyone under her roof.

**The lead.** Ursi Feuz, 52, worked the rescue winch for twenty years until her knee ended it. She wants the hut for good, but the alpine club gave her one trial year and every death counts against her. She reads guests as she read casualties: fast, by what does not fit. She talks short and counts twice.

**The cast.** Res von Allmen, 57, supply-helicopter pilot and her old rescue partner. Nora Gilgen, 22, hut assistant and sharp-eyed geology student. Wachtmeister Beat Zumbrunn, valley police sergeant, mostly a radio voice. Köbi Brawand, 58, the guide who thinks the hut should be his.

**The series engine.** Each book opens with one page of the hut book: every name that will be shut in. The weather closes, a guest dies, and the killer is on that page. Ursi works from what a hut holds: boot rack, bed plan, radio log. New strangers walk up every week with their quarrels.

**The four-book arc.**
1. *The August Page* — High summer: two guides and nine clients are stormbound, a Basel notary dies below the terrace, and Zumbrunn starts trusting Ursi.
2. *The Closing Party* — Autumn closing: fourteen wedding guests are snowed in, the bride's godfather dies beside a blocked stove flue, and Köbi, cleared, owes Ursi.
3. *The Winter Room* — February: Ursi finds five ski tourers where six signed in; the sixth is buried outside, transceiver switched off, and Nora becomes her legs.
4. *The Hut Book* — June reopening: six filmmakers come for the Morf story, their sound recordist dies, the 1961 volume vanishes, and Ursi wins a five-year lease.

*Running thread:* who signs the hut book "E. Morf" every August, in the same hand since 1961, unseen by any warden; book 4 finds a family keeping a dead man alive on paper.

**Why readers come back.** A complete puzzle in every book, solvable from clues on the page, no cliffhanger, no gore. Rösti on the stove, rope and weather done right, and a capable woman of 52 doing a hard job well. Four books, one hut year.

**What makes it different.** The suspect list is an object: the hut book page that opens each volume. The detective is the host, not police or visitor, and her duty of care covers the killer too.
