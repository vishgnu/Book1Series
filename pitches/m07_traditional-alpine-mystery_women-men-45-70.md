# The Alpine Hut Mysteries

**Genre:** Traditional mystery (Alpine setting) · **Readers:** Women and men 45–70 · **Series:** four books, 60,000–70,000 words each, one complete case per book

**Logline.** At a Swiss mountain hut, warden Ursi Feuz, 52, must find the killer among the names in the hut book before police can climb up, while feeding and bunking every guest, the killer included.

**The setting.** The Schwarzhorn Hut, 2,740 meters up in the Bernese Oberland, has 34 bunks, one stove, one radio. Guests walk up four hours and sign the hut book, which never leaves the hut. When weather shuts path and helicopter, police are a day away.

**The lead.** Ursi worked the rescue winch twenty years, until her knee gave out. The Aarwald Mountain Club gave her one trial year: a death ruled accidental ends it, a murder does not. Because murder would save her, she logs the accident evidence first. In 2019 she trusted a radioed count of five on a ledge; the sixth died overnight. Now she counts heads herself, at dark: "Guests lie about fitness and boots."

**The cast.** Res von Allmen, supply pilot, brings her the crossword; he flew in 2019 and never said who radioed the count. Nora Gilgen, 23, hut assistant, quit medical school and argues about everything but evidence. Wachtmeister Beat Zumbrunn needs her log, or the prosecutor rules accident. Vreni Morf, 84, makes cheese on the alp below; her brother Emil vanished on a climb in 1961.

**The series engine.** Every case seals the hut with one body and at most eleven guests. Ursi proves murder from boot rack, bed plan and radio log. She can hold no one, only log. Cases keep arriving because the hut book's 1961 page records a private deal giving the Morfs the alp, and each victim stood between someone and that page.

**The four-book arc.**
1. *Death on the August Page* — August storm: a notary who came to read the 1961 page lies below the terrace, boots dry on the rack.
2. *The Winter Room* — October closing party: the club treasurer, who kept a copy of the deal, dies in the winter room, its flue capped a day early.
3. *Level Four* — March, avalanche level four: guide Lukas Morf, Vreni's great-nephew and heir, lies buried, transceiver off, though Ursi's door check logged it sending.
4. *The Last Signature* — June fog before the club votes on Ursi: an archivist sent to copy the 1961 page dies on the cellar stairs, and the volume vanishes.

*Running thread:* "E. Morf" has been signed every August since Emil vanished, in the same hand, shakier each decade. Book 4 names the signer, shows whose 2019 count failed, and settles whether Vreni keeps her alp and Ursi the hut.

**Why readers come back.** Every puzzle is solvable from the page, without gore. Good company: Res's crossword, Nora's arguments, rösti on the stove. Each case brings Vreni one fact nearer to her brother.

**What makes it different.** The suspect list is a page in the hut book, and the detective cooks breakfast for the person she is about to name.
