# Off by One

**Genre:** Cozy science fiction (space) · **Readers:** Women and men 30–60 · **Series:** four books, 60,000–70,000 words each, one complete case per book

**Logline.** The register at Pell's Orchard says 212; census taker Nell Abara, 53, counts 213 and puts the kettle on. Each book she rights one habitat's wrong number, while Junia Vasko keeps turning up counted twice.

**The setting.** The Outer Reaches: 2,300 habitats, orchard rings and sixty-person ice drums. Each ten-year census, every person must be seen and asked: the last orbital census counted an empty drum as a town and cut a living town's air. Air and Assembly seats follow the headcount: under four million the Reaches lose seats, and the margin is 3,000.

**The lead.** Nell Abara, twenty-nine years unbribed. She wants her pocket book, one stroke per person, to balance. Counting people spares her staying with them: her late husband Ade asked her to stop; she took one more district. Home is the cutter *Tally* and the galley he fitted for two. She asks twice: "I don't doubt you. I count you."

**The cast.** Davi Serrano, 22, apprentice, raised in the town whose air was cut, sure better math would have saved it. Registrar Imogen Park, 61, trained Nell and needs big totals. Oksana Bright, 67, mail-barge skipper, knows who writes to whom, wants a table to return to.

**The series engine.** Park sends the *Tally* wherever count and register disagree. Nell and Davi work through kitchens, ration books, who sings at whose funeral. Every wrong number has a sound reason; every case ends with the count made right, even when right costs.

**The four-book arc.**
1. *One Heartbeat Over* — Pell's Orchard, an apple ring: the 213th is a tug hand declared dead, because reviving him bills eleven years of air. The town splits the bill 213 ways. Oksana rearranges Ade's galley; Nell puts it back.
2. *Forty Quiet Names* — Saint Brine, a salt drum, draws air for forty dead; Hollis Brandt's goats breathe it. Davi's sums catch what Nell's knocking missed; Oksana's undelivered mail confirms it; Nell wins a livestock allowance.
3. *The Uncounted House* — Quiet Harbor admits to ninety former debtors and bakes for ninety-four; a ledger once sold them, so four children go unwritten. Davi asks instead of calculating; Nell counts strokes, no names. Oksana stays for supper.
4. *A Town Under Way* — The hauler *Marigold Parish*, 611 aboard, asks to be counted as a town; an honest ruling risks the four million. Park asks Nell, once, to round up; Nell rules straight; the seats hold by forty.

*Running thread:* Midwife Junia Vasko, 41, was seen and asked twice in one week, a district apart. "I answered true both times." Strike either entry and a drum loses its midwife. Books 1–3 rule out twin, forgery and miscount; book 4's answer sat on a book 1 table: the extra place setting Nell noticed.

**Why readers come back.** A warm galley, a fair puzzle, a society small enough to learn by name. In book 4 Nell leaves the galley as Oksana arranged it, second chair filled.

**What makes it different.** The detective is a census taker whose only power is to knock and ask; her oath: everyone counts, once. The stakes are air and votes, never murder.
