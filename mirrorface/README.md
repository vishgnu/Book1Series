# The Mirror Face

Development material for an original psychological drama — a slow-burn
two-hander about emotional masking, self-deception and the failure of
connection between two people who are too alike to reach each other.
Conceived with trilogy/series potential.

## Premise

Two high-performing professionals wear the same mask for the same reason. One
of them realises it and tries to reach the person underneath the other's. The
reaching is exactly what the mask is built to repel.
