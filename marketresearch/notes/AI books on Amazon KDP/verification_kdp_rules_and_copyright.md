# Verification against primary sources: KDP rules, US copyright, EU/German law and tax

- **Date accessed:** 2026-11-14
- **Scope:** Report section "Amazon allows AI if you disclose it, and copyright protects only the human part" (all subsections) and the "German and EU admin" paragraph.
- **Method:** Pages fetched with `curl -sL -A 'Mozilla/5.0'` and converted to text locally; JS-rendered ACX help pages rendered with Playwright (Chromium); PDFs (Copyright Office report, IRS treaty table, Commission Article 50 guidelines) extracted with pdf.js. EUR-Lex returned a "temporarily not fully available" page for the AI Act, so the official Official Journal text of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 and of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 was taken from the EU Publications Office repository (`publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/...`), which serves the same OJ document. WebSearch was used only to locate primary URLs; nothing is marked CONFIRMED on the basis of a search summary.
- **Status key:** CONFIRMED = read on the authority's own page. CORRECTED = primary gives a different value (right value given). CONTRADICTED = primary says the opposite. NOT FOUND ON PRIMARY = authority's page checked and does not say it. PRIMARY UNREACHABLE = could not open. NOT A PRIMARY-CHECKABLE CLAIM = the claim is about third-party reports, so there is no authority page to check.

## Biggest changes since the report's sources

1. **Kindle Unlimited has been renamed Kindle+** (Amazon, article updated 22 October 2026; US price now $11.99/month). Every KDP help page now says "Kindle+". The report's "Kindle Unlimited" wording is outdated throughout.
2. **The upload cap is no longer "3 titles per day".** KDP now limits **creation to 2 titles per book format per week and submission to 2 new titles per book format per week**, reset Sunday 00:00 UTC.
3. **Kindle Translate now runs in both directions between English and five languages** (Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese), and the source book may be in any of those six languages. It is still an invite-only beta.
4. **Virtual Voice does not support German.** Supported primary languages are English, Spanish, Italian and French, and the beta is US marketplace only.
5. **The EU Commission's Article 50 guidelines expressly list "AI-generated fantasy novels" as outside Article 50(4).** They also define a commercial self-publisher as a professional deployer.
6. **The Digital Omnibus is law:** Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 was published in the OJ on 24 July 2026. The Article 50(2) grace period ends on **2 December 2026**. The 2 February 2027 date is wrong.
7. **KDP bans links to web forms that request customer information (e.g. email address)** in its Kindle content-quality guide. This bears directly on the report's advice to "put a link to an email-list sign-up in every book" (see New findings, item 6).

## New findings (items not retrieved before)

1. **"Disappointing content" (Guide to Kindle Content Quality, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200952510).** "We do not allow content that disappoints our customers or creates a poor shopping experience, including but not limited to:" content marketed as a subscription or redirecting readers elsewhere for the full content; content "freely available on the web"; content "whose primary purpose is to solicit or advertise"; "Content that is not significantly different from content in another book available in the Kindle Store"; "Content that is too short"; "Content that is poorly translated"; "Content that does not provide an enjoyable reading experience"; "Bonus content that appears before a book's primary content"; "Content that is excessively reused, recycled, or repeated within or across books"; "Content that is rebranded or re-released with significant changes to the metadata". The same guide says excessive "Distracting or Destructive Issues" (typos, formatting) can mean removal from sale or "a temporary quality warning displayed on the detail page". The Content Guidelines (G200672390) link here: "We also do not allow content that's typically disappointing to customers." These are the categories that low-effort AI output trips over most (too short, repetitive, poorly translated, not significantly different).

2. **Summaries, workbooks and companions.** Same guide: "Companion guides (including summaries, study guides, workbooks, analyses, and other related content based on other books) are not allowed, with limited exceptions for guides with positive reader engagement." Content Guidelines: "we do not allow companion books based on copyrighted works (e.g., summaries, study guides, etc.) to be published outside the U.S. without written permission from the copyright holder."

3. **Impersonation and misleading metadata (Metadata Guidelines, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560).** Prohibited in titles: "Unauthorized reference to other titles or authors", "Unauthorized reference to a trademarked term", "References that suggest an individual or entity has contributed to or been involved with your book that has not". Author field: "You are free to use a pen name, as long as it does not impair customers' ability to make good buying decisions. If the primary contributor's name is similar to a popular author's name, consider using a pen name, middle name, or adding an initial." Keywords may not reference "Other authors" or "Books by other authors". "Covers that may be misleading because they closely resemble another book's layout, color scheme, fonts and/or images aren't allowed." Book descriptions may not contain "Reviews, quotes, or testimonials" or "Requests for customer reviews". Content Guidelines: "We do not allow descriptive content meant to mislead customers or that doesn't accurately represent the content of the book."

4. **KDP Select exclusivity terms (https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GD9PMU58BV24QFZ7, G200798990).** "You must have exclusive rights for the primary content… Content that is public domain is not eligible. Your Kindle eBook must also be exclusive to the Kindle Store for the KDP Select enrollment period. During the 90-day enrollment period, the Kindle eBook can only be distributed through KDP and public libraries. However, you can continue to distribute print, video, or other formats of your title elsewhere." Enrollment auto-renews ("automatically renewed unless you turn off automatic renewal"). Cancellation is possible within "three days from the start of your enrollment period". Exclusivity extends to Virtual Voice audio: "audiobooks enrolled in KDP Select must be exclusive to Audible for the KDP Select enrollment period. This requirement applies to any audio edition of that book." Benefits: Kindle+ inclusion, the KDP Select Global Fund (paid per first-time page read *or listened*), and one Kindle Countdown Deal or Free Book Promotion per 90-day term. Royalties-in-Kindle+ page (G201541130): "We do not permit authors to offer or participate in marketing that incentivizes Kindle+ customers to read… in exchange for compensation of any kind", including "bonus content, entry to a contest or sweepstakes". The KDP footer shows "Total KDP Select Author Earnings October 2026: $71.1 Million".

5. **Kindle+ (renamed Kindle Unlimited).** Amazon article updated 22 Oct 2026, https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/what-is-kindle-plus: "Kindle+ gives you access to millions of eBooks, audiobooks, comics, manga, and magazines for $11.99 per month plus taxes"; not included with Prime; available in Germany among other countries. KDP: "Audiobooks in Kindle+ are available for distribution in the US marketplace only at this time."

6. **Email-list links: a conflict with the report's recommendation.** Guide to Kindle Content Quality, "Links" section, examples of *prohibited external links*: "Links to commercial eBook store sites other than Amazon" and "**Links to web forms that request customer information (e.g., email address, physical address or similar)**". The same section warns that "the addition of disruptive content that takes readers away from the main content… can result in a poor customer experience… up to and including termination." The report's plan says "Put a link to an email-list sign-up in every book from book one." Read literally, a direct link to a sign-up form is prohibited. Many authors link to an author website instead, but this page does not say whether that is allowed. The plan should be reworded and the question checked with KDP before publishing.

7. **Kindle Translate details.** Source must be live, reflowable, not public domain, not already a translation, under about 900K characters, not a pre-order. "Direct editing of the translated text is not available at this time." Translations are "held to the same content quality guidelines as your source eBook", and the source eBook is "locked for editing until translations are live". There is an option to auto-translate into new languages as they are added. Amazon claims "thousands of authors" and "4+ stars on average", which is Amazon's own unverified claim.

8. **Virtual Voice details.** It is free "at this time", has "80+ virtual voice options", and is US marketplace only. Primary language must be English, Spanish, Italian or French (no German). It is not available if an Audible/ACX audiobook already exists for that edition. "Your audiobook will be held to the same content quality guidelines as your eBook."

9. **EU Commission Art. 50 Guidelines on audiobooks.** Listed as *not* a deep fake: "AI voice replication for fictional characters (e.g., for audiobooks, games or animation) when there is no deception as to the identity of the narrators." The human-review exception requires "deliberate examination of the substance… Fact-checking the accuracy of the content is a minimum requirement", and "spell-checking or grammatical correction… cannot fulfil the conditions". The person holding editorial responsibility should be identified publicly, e.g. "in a publication's colophon or edition notice". This matters only for public-interest non-fiction.

10. **US Copyright Office status.** Part 2 was published 29 January 2025. Its conclusion says "existing legal doctrines are adequate", "Prompts alone, however, at this stage are unlikely to satisfy those requirements", and further registration guidance and a Compendium update are promised. Nothing newer on copyrightability is listed at copyright.gov/ai as of 2026-11-14.
