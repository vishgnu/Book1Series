# KDP categories, formats and realistic earnings for new self-publishers (2025-2026, AI in production)

Method caveat for the report writer: WebFetch could not reach writtenwordmedia.com, allianceindependentauthors.org, alphaxiv.org or kdp.amazon.com (hosts not in the session's allowed network domains; they would need to be allowed to read primary sources). Everything below comes from web-search result summaries, not from reading the full primary pages. Figures are therefore second-hand unless stated, and should be verified against the primary pages linked. Today is 2026-11-14.

## 1. What do large surveys say about self-published income (median vs top tier, new authors, AI users)?

### Takeaway
Self-reported surveys of engaged indie authors show a heavily skewed distribution: a large share earn $100/month or less, while a minority earn thousands. The most-cited median (about $13,500/yr, ALLi 2025) applies to a screened, mostly full-time author group and is not a new-author figure. I found no survey data specific to authors using AI.

### Cited Findings
- Written Word Media 2025 indie author survey: 1,346 responses; 77% self-published, 17% hybrid, 5% traditionally published. Self-reported, skews toward serious, prolific, commercial-fiction indies (romance, fantasy, sci-fi overrepresented). — [Written Word Media 2025 survey](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/) (as summarized by search; page not fetched)
- Same survey, via secondary source: 44% earn $100 or less per month; 13% earn over $5,000/month; 8% over $10,000/month (bands overlap). Secondary-source figures, not verified against the original. — [Written Word Media 2025](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results/)
- Same survey: Amazon was the top revenue source for 83% of respondents. — [search summary of Written Word Media 2025 data via Naomi Jane Substack](https://naomijane.substack.com/p/low-and-medium-content-books-on-amazon)
- Same survey, via an analysis: roughly 80% of authors with 1-3 books earn under $100/month; authors earning $5,000-$7,500/month had a median catalogue of about 30 titles. Secondary; unverified. — [Written Word Media 2025](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results/)
- ALLi 2025 Indie Author Income Survey: reported median annual income $13,500 (up about 6% year-on-year) for a qualifying population (authors spending over half their working time on writing/publishing or earning over half their income from it). Earlier ALLi median: $12,749 (labelled 2022 in some sources, 2023 in others; year label inconsistent). — [ALLi "Truth About Self-Publishing in 2026"](https://www.allianceindependentauthors.org/?p=483); [ALLi 2025 income survey page](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/2025-author-income-survey/)
- One secondary source reports a mean above $80,000 in the same ALLi sample (huge mean/median gap = top-heavy). Unverified. — [ALLi](https://www.allianceindependentauthors.org/?p=483)
- Conflict: one aggregator attributes the $13,500 median to Written Word Media, while ALLi reports it as its own figure. Treat attribution to ALLi as more likely. — [BestWriting stats roundup](https://bestwriting.com/how-much-do-authors-make)
- 2024 Written Word Media survey: average author had about 8 books, varying widely by income bracket. — [Written Word Media 2024](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2024-indie-author-survey-results/)
- A stats roundup claims ALLi says 75% of self-published authors earn under $1,000/year; I could not confirm this attribution. Do not use. — [BestWriting](https://bestwriting.com/self-publishing-statistics)
- Written Word Media's 2026 survey was open at last check, with results expected before end of 2026. — [ALLi news](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/written-word-media-opens-2026-indie-author-survey/)

### Inferences
- The ALLi median is for committed, largely full-time authors, so a typical first-year or first-three-book author is probably well below it; the Written Word Media 1-3 book cohort (about 80% under $100/month, secondary) is the better proxy for new authors.
- Survey populations are selection-biased toward engaged authors who buy marketing services (Written Word Media sells promotion), so true all-KDP-publisher medians are likely lower.

### Gaps
- Authors Guild income survey and K-lytics income data were not retrieved. 
- No survey data found splitting income by AI use. 
- Primary reports not read directly (network block).

## 2. Category-by-category viability vs saturation

### Takeaway
Evidence favors series genre fiction (romance, romantasy, mystery/thriller, LitRPG/cozy fantasy) as where money concentrates, but also where competition is heaviest; low-content and AI-built illustrated/activity books are described as crowded with weak hard earnings data. I found no solid independent earnings data for AI children's picture books or cozy mystery specifically.

### Cited Findings
- Romance is the highest-selling Kindle category; K-lytics-derived summary says top-100 romance titles average rank about 93 (about 641 daily sales estimated); romance is about 55% of top-100 Kindle titles vs mystery/thriller/suspense about 33%. — [AuthorMedia/K-lytics zeitgeist](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-romance-dominates-kindle-but-shows-signs-of-maturing/)
- Jane Friedman reports romance's share of Kindle bestseller lists is loosening, with mystery/thriller/suspense gaining. — [Jane Friedman](https://janefriedman.com/romances-grip-on-kindle-bestseller-lists-is-loosening/)
- K-lytics via AuthorMedia: cozy fantasy and LitRPG described as hottest niches for 2026; romantasy a main driver of fantasy, with dungeon crawler/LitRPG added. — [AuthorMedia](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-cozy-fantasy-and-litrpg-surge-as-hottest-niches-in-2026/)
- Cozy mystery: no standalone sales data found; appears as crossover ("cozy" mixed into paranormal/fantasy searches). — [AuthorMedia](https://www.authormedia.com/zeitgeist-mystery-thriller-suspense-k-lytics-report/)
- Low-content: described as a gold rush that became crowded 2023-2025, with medium-content books seen as better; one vendor page cites Bowker that US ISBN output rose about 32.5% in 2025 to over four million (vendor source, verify with Bowker). — [Naomi Jane Substack](https://naomijane.substack.com/p/low-and-medium-content-books-on-amazon); [WisePPC](https://wiseppc.com/blog/best-selling-low-content-books-amazon-kdp/)
- KDP Builder 2026 report: about 70% of AI-built books in its tool are illustrated/activity/low-content formats; the report states it measures what people build, not sales or earnings. Vendor data. — [KDP Builder](https://kdpbuilder.com/reports/state-of-ai-self-publishing-2026)
- Anecdote with a number: one Substack reports a publisher making over $18k across four years with low-content books. Anecdote. — [Naomi Jane Substack](https://naomijane.substack.com/p/low-and-medium-content-books-on-amazon)
- Amazon's algorithm tweak in 2025 reportedly hurt low-content listings (winners and losers); not verified. — [The Write Moves](https://the-write-moves.beehiiv.com/p/amazon-s-algorithm-tweak-of-2025-winners-and-losers-in-low-content-listings)
- Unsourced/low-reliability claims seen and not to be used: "+180% low-content uploads, -28% royalty per unit" and niche earnings of "$200-$1,200+/month" — [Gen Z blog](https://thefrugal-genz-usa.blogspot.com/2026/04/us-gen-zs-2026-blueprint-ai-powered.html); [passive income blog](https://passive-income-tools.pages.dev/blog/amazon-kdp-passive-income-2026)

### Inferences
- Genre series earn because readers binge and Kindle Unlimited pays per page read, rewarding long books and series; this favors volume in a few genres, not one-off low-content products.
- Many "how-to-earn" pages here are vendor or course-adjacent (Vappingo, Inkfluence, WisePPC, Kindlepreneur affiliate links) with conflicts of interest.

### Gaps
- No verified data on nonfiction how-to/self-help earnings, children's picture books, puzzle/coloring books, thriller-specific or cozy-specific income.
- K-lytics original reports not accessed.

## 3. Royalty mechanics

### Takeaway
Ebook: 35% or 70% options; 70% requires a price band (historically $2.99-$9.99) with a per-MB delivery fee. A reported July 2026 change widens the band to $12.99, but I could not confirm it on Amazon's pages. KENP payouts run about $0.004-$0.005 per page, derived from a monthly fund.

### Cited Findings
- 70% option: delivery fee $0.15/MB in US; 35% option has no delivery fee. Own arithmetic from source: a $9.99 book at 1 MB is about $6.89 at 70% vs $3.50 at 35%. — [KDP help](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200644210); [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=11884)
- Price band: historically $2.99-$9.99 for 70%; two secondary sources say effective July 7, 2026 it becomes $2.99-$12.99 on Amazon.com. Unverified against Amazon. — [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=11884); [Writeo](https://writeo.app/news/amazon-kdp-royalty-cap-12-99-july-2026)
- Paperback royalty = list price x 60% minus printing cost (varies by page count and marketplace); no current printing-cost figures retrieved. — [Author Imprints](https://www.authorimprints.com/amazon-kdp-royalty-pricing/)
- KENP rate = month's marketplace fund / total KENP read; Amazon announces the fund around the 15th of the following month; Amazon does not publish a fixed rate. — [KDP help](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G5HHMLVVEW48MQYW)
- Third-party trackers: April 2026 about $0.00482/page; May 2026 about $0.00489; 2026 range $0.00420-$0.00482 (conflicting); Global Fund $70.3M May 2026, $61.7M Feb 2026; 2025 typically about $0.00452-$0.00465, with October 2025 at $0.005007. Sources conflict, so no firm YoY trend. — [profitable.app](https://profitable.app/kdp/stats/kenp-rate); [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=11890)
- KDP Select page lists total earnings of $69.3M for September 2026. — [KDP Select](https://kdp.amazon.com/self-publishing/KDPSelect)
- KDP Virtual Voice audiobooks (beta): list price $3.99-$14.99; 40% royalty on a la carte sales; Audible Plus royalties separate. One blog cites a 15% figure for a different channel; sources conflict. — [KDP help](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GHJW2N8GLTQLK9TY); [Bookseller](https://thebookseller.com/news/kdp-tests-virtual-voice-narrated-audiobooks-in-the-us)
- Kindle Translate: announced Nov 6, 2025, free, beta English-Spanish and German-English; expanded June 30, 2026 to French, Italian, Portuguese; titles labelled "Kindle Translate"; eligible for KDP Select/Kindle Unlimited. — [Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/md/news/books-and-authors/amazon-kindle-translate-books-authors); [Publishers Lunch](https://lunch.publishersmarketplace.com/2025/11/amazon-launches-ai-translation-for-self-published-ebooks/)
- One source claims KENP page reads dropped with Kindle+ in 2026; not verified. — [Inkfluence](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/kdp-kenp-page-reads-drop-kindle-plus-2026)

### Inferences
- At about $0.0045/page, a 300-page KENP-length read is about $1.35 per full read, below a 70% royalty on a $4.99 ebook (about $3.4), so KU favors high-volume series readers.

### Gaps
- Exact KDP print cost tables and the official KENP history table not retrieved. 
- No evidence found on AI-narrated or AI-translated audiobook/ebook earnings.

## 4. Evidence on AI-generated books succeeding or failing commercially

### Takeaway
The best quantitative evidence is a 2026 academic preprint: AI-heavy genre fiction is about 20% of titles but about 11-12% of revenue. No documented case studies with verified numbers of an AI-farm book succeeding were found. Amazon responded with disclosure rules and a 3-titles/day cap.

### Cited Findings
- Preprint (arXiv-style, as reported): AI detection on 14,419 self-published genre-fiction books (2023 to June 2026) matched to daily sales from a panel covering about 95% of daily ebook sales. Substantial-AI books: 20% of books, 12.1% of sales, 11.3% of revenue; no-AI books: 62.9% of books, 71.7% of sales, 72.5% of revenue. Titles with sales grew 19.2x vs revenue 8.9x (per-title revenue falling). Not independently verified; I could not open the paper. — [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/ai-generated-books-are-flooding-amazon-and-tanking-sales-for-human-authors/); [alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2607.20349.md)
- Reports note Amazon said its internal metrics did not show that growth, and would not share figures. — [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/study-ai-books-gain-amazon-share-as-catalog-outpaces-revenue)
- Amazon cap of 3 titles/day, after fake AI books under Jane Friedman's name and dangerous foraging guides; AI content must be disclosed, but verification is unclear. (Date of the policy not confirmed in results; reporting is from 2023.) — [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/100246-amazon-puts-cap-self-publishing-combat-flood-ai.html)
- No source using the term "book farms" and no NYT/WSJ/Guardian primary article retrieved.

### Inferences
- Per-title earnings for AI-heavy books are below human-written peers on average, but the study measures averages and cannot say a disciplined, human-edited workflow fails.

### Gaps
- No named case studies with disclosed numbers for AI-produced books. 
- Original preprint methodology not reviewed.

## 5. Time and titles to first meaningful income

### Takeaway
No verified source gives a standard timeline. Survey proxies suggest meaningful monthly income correlates with tens of titles, not a few.

### Cited Findings
- Older Written Word Media data via a Goodreads blog (year not verified, older than 2024): $250-$999/month authors averaged about 17 books; $1,000-$4,999/month about 29 books. — [Goodreads blog](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/20735990-self-publishing-survey)
- 2025 survey proxy: authors with 1-3 books mostly under $100/month (see section 1). — [Written Word Media 2025](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results/)
- Vappingo's "$800-$3,000/month for 4-10 books" is an unsourced estimate (vendor); disregard. — [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/how-much-do-kdp-authors-earn/)
- No reliable survey establishes a timeline to $10,000/month. — [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/10000-month-kdp/)

### Inferences
- Realistic expectation from survey proxies: first $100/month needs a few titles plus marketing; $1,000/month tends to coincide with roughly 20-30 titles (older, self-reported data).

### Gaps
- Time-in-years data and verified disclosed case studies not found (r/KDP, 20BooksTo50K not searched).
