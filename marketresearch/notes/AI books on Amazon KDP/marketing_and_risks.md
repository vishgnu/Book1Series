# Marketing, discovery and risks of publishing AI-assisted books on Amazon KDP (2025-2026)

**Method caveat for the report writer.** In this session the primary-source hosts (kdp.amazon.com, ftc.gov, authorsguild.org, publishersweekly.com, vappingo.com, alphaxiv.org) did not resolve for WebFetch, so I could not open them. Today is 2026-11-14.

## Discovery: algorithm, categories, new-release visibility, Kindle Unlimited, series vs standalone

### Takeaway
I found no authoritative description of Amazon's ranking algorithm; what exists is practitioner lore. The best-supported points are that Kindle Unlimited pays a variable per-page rate (about $0.0042-$0.0050 in 2025) and that income correlates with series and catalog size (correlation, self-reported).

### Cited Findings
- KDP Select/KU pays from a monthly Global Fund divided by total KENP pages read, so the per-page rate floats. One tracker puts 2025 at about $0.00452-$0.00465 per page; another lists May-Sept 2025 at about $0.00419-$0.00452; a third lists October 2025 at $0.005007 with a $61.2M fund; one lists $0.00482 for April 2026. Trackers disagree on fund size, so check Amazon's monthly figure. — [ScribeCount KENP tool](https://scribecount.com/tools/kenp-earnings-estimator); [BookBeam KENP rate guide](https://bookbeam.io/blog/amazon-kdp-kenp-rate-guide/); [profitable.app KENP rate](https://profitable.app/kdp/stats/kenp-rate); [profitable.app global fund](https://profitable.app/kdp/stats/global-fund); official page: [KDP help G201541140](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G201541140) (not opened)
- Written Word Media 2025 indie author survey: income correlates with larger catalogs and series; authors with 10+ books and an engaged email list were most likely to earn full-time income. The survey itself warns the sample is self-selected, self-reported, and correlation is not causation (practitioner source, via secondary summary). — [Written Word Media 2025 survey](https://www.writtenwordmedia.com/2025-indie-author-survey-results-insights-into-self-publishing-for-authors/amp/)
- Alliance of Independent Authors 2025 survey, per a summary: median self-published author income $13,500 (self-reported). — [ALLi](https://wp.allianceindependentauthors.org/?p=483)
- Claims that series earn "3-5x" or "40-80%" more per reader come from vendor blogs with no cited dataset; do not rely on them. — [Vappingo series vs standalone](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/book-series-sell-better-standalone/)
- A Book Riot piece argues AI-generated uploads flood search and make human self-published authors harder to find, and questions whether Kindle Unlimited can survive AI. — [Book Riot](https://bookriot.com/can-kindle-unlimited-survive-ai/)

### Inferences
- Because KU pays per page read from a fixed pool, a flood of AI titles enrolled in KU dilutes the pool; I have not found data proving this specifically.
- The series advantage is plausible (read-through) but the evidence is correlational.

### Gaps
- No primary Amazon documentation of also-boughts, category/keyword mechanics or new-release visibility windows (e.g. the "30-day new release" boost) was retrievable. No independent data on how many sales a new title needs to rank.
- No peer-reviewed data on series vs standalone causal effect.

## Amazon Ads: CPC, ACoS, test budgets, how often beginners lose money

### Takeaway
No reliable current benchmark exists; vendors themselves say to use your own data. Break-even math shows beginners lose money whenever average CPC exceeds royalty x conversion rate. I found no source quantifying how often beginners lose.

### Cited Findings
- Rough genre CPC averages from one guide: about $0.20-$0.40 romance/mystery, $0.40-$0.80 fantasy/thriller (practitioner, unsourced). — [PublishDrive guide](https://www.publishdrive.com/amazon-ads-for-authors); [River Editor 2026 guide](https://rivereditor.com/guides/how-to-use-amazon-ads-for-books-2026)
- Break-even example: a $3.49 royalty at 8% conversion supports max CPC of about $0.28; a $2.99 book at 8% about $0.17. Above that you lose money on that title's ads alone. — [Vappingo, Amazon Ads and pricing](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=11834)
- Suggested ACoS targets: 30-50% for a standalone; break-even ACoS roughly equals royalty percentage (about 35% at the 35% royalty tier). — [Vappingo ACoS guide](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=11769)
- ACoS understates value for series because it ignores read-through to later books. — [ScribeCount ads dashboard](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/troubleshooting-author-resources/amazon-ads-dashboard)
- Vappingo's 2026 guide warns fixed CPC benchmarks go stale and recommends using your own data. Sponsored Brands: Amazon Ads reportedly announced 6 June 2025 that it is open to all authors, though some guides still say three titles are required (conflict). — [Vappingo budgets](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/?p=11773); [Reedsy](https://blog.reedsy.com/amazon-ads-for-authors)
- Consultant advice: build an email list before spending on Facebook/Amazon ads, which need budget and learning time; another practitioner says Facebook and Amazon ads "still work". — [Self-Publishing Advice podcast](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/ricardo-fayet-on-marketing/)

### Inferences
- With CPC around $0.30-$0.50 and low conversion for an unknown author, a few hundred dollars of tests can easily produce no profitable ad set. This is arithmetic from the cited break-even example, not measured data.

### Gaps
- No source found for "minimum realistic test budget" other than a 2017 $5/day blog example with no results. No data on share of beginners who lose money.

## Other channels: newsletters, promo sites, BookBub, BookTok, Facebook

### Takeaway
BookBub Featured Deals are costly (hundreds of dollars) and competitive; other channels' costs were not found with citable figures.

### Cited Findings
- BookBub says it receives hundreds of submissions daily and selects about 25%; third parties claim 4-8% or 10-20% (conflict). Submission is free; payment is due only if accepted. — [BookBub Insights](https://insights.bookbub.com/?p=65); [Reedsy BookBub guide](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/bookbub/)
- Third-party 2025 fiction price estimates: free Women's Fiction about $698; free Crime about $783; Mystery/Thriller $940 free/$790 at $0.99; SF $780/$640; Fantasy $670/$570. Official chart not seen. — [Writers Helping Writers, April 2025](https://writershelpingwriters.net/2025/04/i-tried-a-bookbub-featured-deal/); [BookBub partner pricing](https://www.bookbub.com/partners/pricing)
- BookTok is split on AI; some creators were reportedly paid by AI companies; a 2025 "AI accusation" episode shows creators can be accused of AI use. — [Ypulse, June 2025](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2025/06/24/gen-z-favorite-booktok-is-going-through-a-wave-of-ai-controversy/); [Good Story Co.](https://goodstoryco.substack.com/p/the-ai-accusation-heard-round-booktok); [The Bookseller](https://www.thebookseller.com/news/books-on-booktok-ai-might-be-able-to-write-a-book-that-sells-but-i-am-not-convinced-it-can-write-a-book-that-matters)

### Inferences
- A BookBub deal usually requires an existing book with decent reviews, which a brand-new AI-assisted author is unlikely to have.

### Gaps
- Nothing found on BookFunnel/StoryOrigin pricing, Freebooksy costs, Facebook ads cost per subscriber, or TikTok conversion evidence.

## Reviews: rules and manipulation

### Takeaway
Amazon bans incentivized reviews (since Oct 2016, except Vine). Review swaps, friends/family reviews and "free ARC for review" are widely described as prohibited; the FTC 2024 rule adds civil penalties.

### Cited Findings
- Oct 2016 ban on incentivized reviews except Vine; removals of large numbers of reviews reported in 2016 (older data). — [Marketplace Pulse](https://www.marketplacepulse.com/articles/incentivized-reviews-will-lead-to-selling-suspension-to-amazon); [Retail Dive 2016](https://www.retaildive.com/news/amazon-cracking-down-on-customer-generated-reviews/431158)
- A 2026 consultant guide lists review swaps, family/friend reviews and incentivized reviews as prohibited; a checker tool claims free ARCs for reviews count as incentivized (commercial, unverified against Amazon text). — [Goat Consulting](https://www.goatconsulting.com/merchandising/amazon-product-review-guidelines); [bookwriter.vip](https://www.bookwriter.vip/amazon-review-policy-checker)
- Amazon suspends, bans or sues violators. — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/one-in-10-amazon-customers-offered-bribes-for-positive-reviews-aUARn0z1qLUu)
- FTC final rule announced Aug 14, 2024, effective Oct 21, 2024: bans fake/AI-generated reviews, paid reviews, undisclosed insider reviews, review suppression, fake social indicators; civil penalties for knowing violations (about $51,744 per violation in 2024 figures). — [FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials); [DWT](https://dwt.com/insights/2024/08/ftc-finalizes-rule-banning-fake-consumer-reviews)

### Gaps
- Amazon's own Community Guidelines text and author-specific ARC wording not retrieved.

## Saturation and competition

### Takeaway
Credible evidence indicates AI text is now in roughly half of new Amazon books and per-book revenue for human self-published titles fell; the study was seen only via summaries.

### Cited Findings
- An NBER-linked study (Reimers & Waldfogel) reportedly finds about half of 2025 books sold on Amazon contain AI text, up from 23% in 2023; revenue per book for 14,000+ self-published titles fell 2023 to 2025 in six of eight genres; modelled consumer surplus gains <1% (2023), >3% (2024), about 7% (2025). Seen only via summaries. — [alphaxiv abstract](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2607.20349.md); [Office Chai](https://officechai.com/ai/half-the-ebooks-published-on-amazon-are-now-written-by-ai/); [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/ai-generated-books-are-flooding-amazon-and-tanking-sales-for-human-authors/)
- Originality.ai analysis of 844 self-help titles found AI in a large majority (figures vary: 77% vs 90% across reports). — [KuCoin summary](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/ai-generated-self-help-books-flood-amazon-report-reveals-77-ai-written-content)
- BookBub poll (April 2025, 1,200+ self-published authors): 45% using AI for writing assistance; PW survey Nov 2025 reported more. — [Office Chai summary above](https://officechai.com/ai/half-the-ebooks-published-on-amazon-are-now-written-by-ai/)
- KDP caps new titles at 3 per day (announced Sept 2023 per most sources; one source says 2024) and requires disclosure of AI-generated content to Amazon, not shown to customers. — [Publishers Weekly](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/digital/content-and-e-books/article/93207-kdp-will-limit-daily-number-of-new-titles.html); [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/); [Jane Friedman](https://janefriedman.com/how-ai-generated-books-could-hurt-self-publishing-authors/)

### Gaps
- Exact title-volume counts and the effect on a new title's visibility are not directly measured in anything I retrieved.

## Reader and market backlash; platform policy

### Takeaway
Surveys show a substantial minority to majority of readers dislike AI books and want disclosure; wording drives the numbers. Other retailers vary from disclosure to rejection.

### Cited Findings
- YouGov/Black Chateau poll (Aug 2025, 1,000 US adults): most want disclosure; 61% less fulfilled after learning a book was AI-written (via secondary report). — [Journal of Cyber Policy](https://journalofcyberpolicy.com/?p=343982)
- Bokbarometern 2026 (Sweden) reportedly: two-thirds negative about AI-written books, 3% positive (secondhand, unverified). — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/pudgycat/two-thirds-of-swedes-reject-ai-books-and-the-3-percent-who-want-them-are-a-statistical-error-4347)
- A vendor blog says about 20% of readers would never buy AI-assisted; no primary source. — [Inkfluence](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/do-readers-care-ai-written-books-2026)
- Apple Books: disclosure model ("AI Generated by" credit). Draft2Digital reportedly turns away most of the AI books it receives; Kobo lists wholly/partially AI-generated among rejection reasons; IngramSpark bars AI-only and requires attribution. Conflicting guides; verify on platform pages. — [Inkfluence store guide](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/which-stores-allow-ai-written-books-2026); [Indie Author Magazine D2D CEO](https://indieauthormagazine.com/draft2digital-ceo-kris-austin-details-how-ai-is-actually-affecting-the-industry/); [Built Written](https://www.builtwritten.com/blog/ai-book-publishing); [Inkfluence IngramSpark](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/ingramspark-ai-policy-2026)
- Account/enforcement: authors report sudden KDP terminations; several vendor guides claim 2026 enforcement sweeps for undisclosed AI (single-source, unconfirmed). The "90% never reinstated" stat has no primary source. — [Writeo](https://writeo.app/news/amazon-kdp-ai-disclosure-enforcement-2026); [Writers Weekly](https://writersweekly.com/angela-desk/amazon-kdp-terminates-another-authors-account-and-keeps-her-royalties); [AuthorMedia](https://www.authormedia.com/why-amazon-is-terminating-innocent-authors/)

### Gaps
- No Pew survey on AI books specifically; no peer-reviewed reader-behavior data; Goodreads/review-flagging prevalence not found. KDP's current policy wording (AI-generated vs AI-assisted definitions) not opened.

## Scams

### Takeaway
FTC action in April 2026 against a major AI-book income course is the strongest documented evidence that "passive income from AI books" pitches are often deceptive.

### Cited Findings
- FTC (Apr 13, 2026): Publishing.com LLC and principals Christian and Rasmus Mikkelsen agreed to pay $1.5M; alleged false earnings claims for AI Publishing Academy and Publishing Accelerator, illusory refund guarantees (some conditioned on positive testimonials), undisclosed employee reviews; most buyers never achieved promised income. — [FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/04/publishingcom-pay-15-million-misleading-consumers-about-how-much-income-they-could-earn-using); [FKKS](https://advertisinglaw.fkks.com/post/102mps2/ftc-charges-publishingcom-with-false-earnings-claims-illusory-refund-guarantees)
- Course priced at about $1,995 per Publishers Marketplace; FTC investigation reported Jan 2025. — [Publishers Marketplace](https://lunch.publishersmarketplace.com/2025/01/ftc-investigates-company-that-teaches-people-to-self-publish-ai-books/)
- Writer Beware (2023): scammers use Amazon trademarks to sell fake "KDP" services to authors. — [Writer Beware](https://writerbeware.blog/2023/07/07/how-scammers-are-using-amazon-and-amazon-trademarks-to-rip-writers-off/)
- Red flags (from FTC allegations): income promises, free-video funnels, refunds conditioned on testimonials, undisclosed paid reviews.

### Gaps
- No Writer Beware post specific to KDP passive-income/AI courses found; no data on fake-review-seller prosecutions specific to books.

## Tax and business basics (US, Germany/EU note)

### Takeaway
KDP withholds 30% by default; non-US authors can claim treaty rates with a W-8BEN and tax ID; Germany is commonly listed at 0% but confirm in the KDP tax profile. German registration/Kleinunternehmer treatment needs a Steuerberater.

### Cited Findings
- KDP default 30% withholding; reduced treaty rate possible with TIN; reduced rate reverts to 30% if W-8 expires. — [KDP help G201274750](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G201274750)
- Several author sources list Germany at 0%; tables conflict for other countries; KDP shows the applicable rate in the tax profile. Royalties from non-US stores (e.g. Amazon.de) reportedly not subject to US withholding. — [Vappingo non-US authors](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-for-non-us-authors/); [SFFWorld treaty article](https://www.sffworld.com/?p=4)
- Germany: Kleinunternehmer may omit VAT but must still file an income tax return; register with the Finanzamt for a tax number; long-running losses can draw scrutiny. — [Selbststaendigkeit.de](https://selbststaendigkeit.de/buchhaltung-fuer-gruender/kleinunternehmer-steuer/); [Finanztip forum](https://www.finanztip.de/community/forum/thema/30861-buchautor/)

### Gaps
- Not resolved: whether KDP income is Gewerbe vs freiberuflich for a German resident, current Kleinunternehmer thresholds, and EU VAT on digital sales. Needs a German tax adviser.
