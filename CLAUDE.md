# The Mirror Face — working context for Claude Code

This repo is a **writing project**, not a codebase. It holds the development
material for an original psychological drama with trilogy/series potential.
Everything is plain markdown. There is nothing to build, lint or test.

## What the project is

Two high-performing professionals wear the same defensive emotional mask. One
recognises it in the other through *self*-recognition (not diagnosis) and tries
to reach the person underneath. The reach is experienced as an intrusive audit,
so the mask hardens. Sameness, not difference, is the obstacle to intimacy.

- **Engine (load-bearing, all versions):** values → exposure → mask → flare → shame.
  Full statement: `outlines/v1-the-mirror-face.md` §2.
- **Two nerves:** A tilts *unvalued* (hunger for recognition), B tilts *audit*
  (hunger for autonomy).
- **The only way through:** the reaching character has to drop his own mask
  first, unilaterally.
- **Names are placeholders:** ADRIAN (A) and BÄR / "B." (B).

## Craft notes established so far

- **Protect the "two hidden ledgers" motif.** Each character keeps concealed
  emotional accounting the other can't see. It is the most structurally
  distinctive device across versions.
- **V2's milieu is saturated; three of its devices are not:** the near-miss
  insight commodified before it can land, total non-catharsis, and the coda
  where the tragedy propagates to an anonymous stranger.
- **V3's question is the sharpest:** does a real self still exist underneath
  the performance?
- **Direction taken:** away from social media as an active plot mechanism (V2),
  toward V3's framing. A V1-spine + V2-mechanism hybrid was considered and set
  aside.
- **Trilogy branches:** humanist (construction → transmission → dissolution)
  suits a novel; systemic (the mask propagating algorithmically across
  strangers) suits series or film.
- **Structural risk to avoid:** covert testing — recognition offered as a
  "communication experiment" the other person could fail.
- **Comp titles used in originality research:** Beef, Good Will Hunting, The
  Sopranos, Persona, In the Mood for Love, Normal People, Mad Men, The Remains
  of the Day, Yasmina Reza's plays.

## Guardrails (from V1 §9 — apply to every draft)

1. A is never the enlightened rescuer. Both are equally masked and triggered.
2. The mask is a disowned need, never strategy. Stuck, not scheming.
3. No resolution through a single warm gesture. Respect the flinch.
4. "Protective mask" must not slide into "the mask is the real me".

V2 adds: no cathartic beat in the ending, and the audience must not be allowed
to feel superior to A and B (`outlines/v2-no-notifications.md` §6).

## How to work in this repo

- **The outlines are the author's source text.** Don't rewrite or "improve"
  them in place unless asked. New work goes in new files.
- **Suggested layout for new material:** `outlines/` for version outlines,
  `treatments/` for narrative treatments, `trilogy/` for Books 2–3, `drafts/`
  for scene- or chapter-level prose, `notes/` for research and evaluations.
- **One change per commit, with a message that says what moved in the story**
  (e.g. "V3: add fourth ending beat sheet"), so the history reads as a
  development log.
- **Use branches for alternatives** (a different ending, a different POV
  scheme) rather than overwriting, so versions can be compared with a diff.
- **Give honest evaluation.** Flag cliché, saturation and anything that breaks
  the guardrails. Don't praise by default.
- Language of the material is English.
