# AI-assisted books for Amazon KDP: tools, production workflow and costs (as of Nov 2026)

**Method caveat for the report writer.** Research date: 2026-11-14. Only web search was available. Every attempt to fetch primary pages (kdp.amazon.com, sudowrite.com and others) failed with DNS errors (hosts outside the session's allowed network domains), so **no official pricing or terms page was read directly**. All figures below come from search-result summaries of third-party pages. Many of those pages are affiliate, coupon or vendor-marketing sites, and prices conflict between them. Treat every price as "reported, unverified against the vendor" and re-check before budgeting. I only list claims the search results actually stated.

## AI writing: general models and dedicated fiction tools

### Takeaway
Reported entry prices are about $20/month for ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro, $19-59/month for Sudowrite, roughly $4-20/month for NovelCrafter (plus pay-as-you-go API costs) and about $10-15/month for NovelAI. Reviews say all of them still need human oversight for consistency and repetition in long fiction, but there is no rigorous benchmark. I found no Gemini subscription price and nothing on Squibler.

### Cited Findings
- Sudowrite has Hobby & Student, Professional and Max tiers plus Enterprise. Most sources list $19, $29 and $59 per month. One outlier lists $10 and $25, which probably reflects annual billing or an older price. Annual billing is reported at roughly $10, $22 and $44 per month, but the same source's annual totals look inconsistent. Credit allowances differ between sources, and the sources conflict on whether credits roll over. Price date: 2026, many pages undated or last checked around July 2026. — [Activepieces/affiliate-style roundup](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/sudowrite-pricing-2026-which-plan-should-you-get.md); [Subger](https://subger.com/en/service/sudowrite); [AffMaven (affiliate)](https://affmaven.com/sudowrite-pricing/)
- NovelCrafter is bring-your-own-API-key, so model usage is paid separately to the provider. Reported tiers are $4 (Scribe), $8 (Hobbyist), $14 (Artisan) and $20 (Specialist) per month (April 2026), with a 21-day trial reported (Feb 2026). — [Jenova review](https://www.jenova.ai/ru/resources/novelcrafter); [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/novelcrafter/pricing); [Makerstack](https://makerstack.co/reviews/novelcrafter-review/)
- NovelAI: Tablet $10/month and Scroll $15/month (listing refreshed May 2026). Higher-tier prices are not clearly published. — [xpay listing](https://www.xpay.sh/saas-pricing/novelai/); [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/novelcrafter/pricing) (see search summary; NovelAI note was in the same result set)
- ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro were both listed at $20/month, verified April 2026 by InkFluence. InkFluence sells a competing AI book tool, so it has a commercial interest. — [InkFluence comparison](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/compare/inkfluence-ai-vs-novelcrafter)
- Sudowrite user feedback reports scene-to-scene inconsistencies in longer fiction and heavy repetition of words and phrases that forces editing of each chapter. — [Sudowrite feedback: consistency](https://feedback.sudowrite.com/p/ai-consistency-and-workflow-limitations-overview); [Sudowrite feedback: repetition](https://feedback.sudowrite.com/p/too-much-repetition)
- An independent test across 70,000+ words reported that Sudowrite still struggles to keep character consistency without human oversight. — [UC Strategies review](https://ucstrategies.com/news/sudowrite-review-i-tested-the-22-month-ai-against-chatgpt-across-70000-words/)
- A Tom's Guide piece reports that ChatGPT alone broke down when writing a novel. I saw only the headline in the results. — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/writing-a-novel-in-2026-heres-why-chatgpt-alone-wont-get-you-to-the-finish-line)
- A ranked roundup (unverified listicle) calls Claude a runner-up for fiction with strong voice consistency over long contexts. Another comparison says general chatbots need manual context management for novels. — [Toolradar roundup](https://toolradar-site.pages.dev/best-ai-fiction-writing-tools-2026-novels-stories); [Chapter](https://chapter-blog.pages.dev/best-ai-for-long-form-writing/)
- Sudowrite's own blog and affiliate sites praise the Story Bible for continuity. This is promotional. — [Sudowrite blog](https://sudowrite.com/blog/best-ai-for-creative-writing-in-2026-tested-and-compared/)

### Inferences
- A lean team can write with one $20 general-model subscription. A comfortable team could add Sudowrite or NovelCrafter for story-bible and continuity tooling.
- NovelCrafter's total cost is the subscription plus API spend, so it is not directly comparable with flat-rate tools.

### Gaps
- No Gemini consumer plan price, no Squibler pricing, and no official pricing pages for any tool above.
- No rigorous benchmark of consistency, repetition or factual error rates across models. Nonfiction factual-error evidence was not found.
- Terms-of-service wording on ownership and commercial use of outputs for each writing tool was not retrieved.

## Covers and illustrations

### Takeaway
Reported pricing is about $10/month for Midjourney, $5-10/month for Firefly and $8-20/month for Ideogram. Firefly is the most often cited as the safest for commercial use. A human cover costs about $880 on average via Reedsy. Amazon's cover creator was not covered.

### Cited Findings
- Midjourney plans start around $10/month. Companies with over $1M gross annual revenue must buy Pro or Mega for commercial use. One source says there is no indemnification. — [Magic Hour roundup](https://magichour.ai/blog/cost-of-ai-image-generators); [Eesel Midjourney pricing](https://eesel.ai/blog/midjourney-pricing)
- Ideogram prices conflict ($20 vs $8-15/month), and sources disagree on whether free-tier outputs are licensed for commercial use. — [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/blog/best-ai-image-generators-2026); [Daily Toolbox](https://getdailytoolbox.pages.dev/ai-tools/adobe-firefly-vs-midjourney-vs-ideogram)
- Adobe Firefly is reported at $5-9.99/month, trained on licensed and public-domain content, and some reports say Adobe indemnifies commercial use. This needs confirming in Adobe's terms. — [Rework comparison](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-design/best-adobe-firefly-alternatives)
- Human covers: Reedsy's analysis of 9,600+ 2025 collaborations gives an average of $880, with most between $625 and $1,250. Fantasy, romance and sci-fi cost more, and nonfiction less. Reedsy sells these services, so it has a commercial interest. — [Reedsy](https://reedsy.com/blog/book-cover-design-cost)

### Inferences
- Lean covers could be AI-generated at about $10/month, but a human-checked cover is safer in genre markets. KDP's AI disclosure applies to AI-generated images (see policy section).

### Gaps
- Amazon's KDP Cover Creator (features, limits, cost) was not found.
- Official licensing terms for Midjourney, Ideogram and Firefly, and US copyrightability of AI images, were not retrieved. Canva and Book Bolt cover tools were not covered.

## Formatting and interior

### Takeaway
Atticus is reported at $147 one-time and cross-platform. Vellum is reported at roughly $200-250 one-time and Mac-only. Reedsy Studio is described as free.

### Cited Findings
- Atticus: one-time $147 (June 2026 source), no subscription, runs on Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebook. One source says there is no free trial. The source also warns the price may change. — [Publishing.co.uk](https://www.publishing.co.uk/guides/how-much-does-atticus-cost/); [Automateed (affiliate-style)](https://automateed.com/atticus-vs-vellum)
- Vellum: Ebooks tier $199.99 and Press tier $249.99 (2026), one-time, Mac only. Many "Vellum 2026" results were crypto or the unrelated Vellum AI product and were ignored. — [Makerstack](https://makerstack.co/reviews/vellum-write-review/); [Publishing.co.uk](https://publishing.co.uk/guides/how-much-does-vellum-cost/)
- Reedsy's writing and formatting app (Reedsy Studio) is listed as free. — [Reedsy ProWritingAid page](https://reedsy.com/blog/prowritingaid/); [AI Tools Atlas](https://aitoolsatlas.ai/compare/reedsy-studio-vs-prowritingaid)

### Gaps
- Kindle Create and Canva status and prices were not found. Book Bolt low-content templates were not covered. Reedsy Studio's export limits were not retrieved.

## Editing and quality

### Takeaway
Grammarly and ProWritingAid cost roughly $10-30/month. Reedsy-derived figures put an 80,000-word human edit at about $1,600-$2,880 per stage. I found nothing on AI-detection concerns.

### Cited Findings
- ProWritingAid: the free editor is limited to 500 words at a time. Paid pricing conflicts: $30/month or $120/year in one tracker, "from $10/month" billed yearly in Toolradar (Oct 2026). — [Techpresso review](https://academy.techpresso.co/reviews/is-prowritingaid-worth-it); [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/prowritingaid); [Reedsy ProWritingAid review](https://reedsy.com/blog/prowritingaid-review/)
- Grammarly has a free tier. Pro is reported at $12 per member per month billed annually or $30 monthly. — [Toolradar comparison](https://toolradar.com/compare/prowritingaid-vs-grammarly)
- Reedsy's August 2026 guide (via a secondary source) gives approximate costs for an 80,000-word book: editorial assessment about $2,000, developmental $2,880, copy edit $2,160, proofread $1,600. Typical all-in self-publishing total reported at $2,940-$5,660. Not confirmed on Reedsy's own page. — [Chapter](https://blog.chapter.pub/reedsy-pricing/)

### Gaps
- Beta reader costs, AI-detector reliability and whether Amazon uses detectors were not found.

## Audio

### Takeaway
KDP Virtual Voice was invite-only beta in 2023, and its current availability is unverified. ACX is reported to require human narration. Findaway/Voices by INaudio reportedly accepts AI narration only from approved partners.

### Cited Findings
- Virtual Voice began as an invite-only beta in 2023 in the US. — [Publishers Weekly](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/audio-books/article/93625-kindle-direct-publishing-will-beta-test-virtual-voice-narrated-audiobooks.html)
- KDP help says the beta author experience is confidential, that outside-Audible audiobooks do not affect eligibility, and that KDP Select exclusivity terms apply. Only snippets were seen. — [KDP help](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GFAQU3LUEHCRB8KD)
- An Oct 2026 report claims Amazon rebranded Kindle Unlimited and made Virtual Voice free for authors. I could not verify this against Amazon. — [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/amazon-rebrands-kindle-unlimited-to-kindle-adds-ai-narrated-audiobooks/story)
- ACX April 15, 2026 requirements reportedly demand human narration unless authorized, and list unauthorized AI text-to-speech as prohibited. Other sources claim the opposite, and those are weaker. — [InkFluence ACX explainer (vendor of an AI tool)](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/ai-narration-audible-acx-policy-2026)
- A September 2026 store-by-store check reports Voices by INaudio (formerly Findaway) accepts only unmodified packages from Google Play Books, ElevenLabs and Spoken, and that Apple takes only its own narration (conflicting with other sources). ElevenLabs reportedly distributes to 40+ retailers directly. — [InkFluence store guide](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/learn/where-to-sell-an-ai-audiobook); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/ai-narration/)

### Gaps
- ElevenLabs plan prices, Virtual Voice royalty terms and the primary ACX text were not retrieved.

## ISBNs, copyright and policy compliance

### Takeaway
Bowker is reported at $125 for one ISBN and $295 for 10. KDP's free ISBN works only on KDP. KDP requires disclosure of AI-generated (not AI-assisted) content.

### Cited Findings
- Bowker US: 1 ISBN $125, 10 for $295, 100 for $575, 1,000 for $1,500, with barcodes sold separately. Older pages show $250 for 10. — [InkFluence ISBN guide](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/do-you-need-an-isbn-2026); [Reedsy ISBN guide](https://reedsy.com/blog/guide/isbn-number/)
- KDP gives free ISBNs for paperbacks and hardcovers. Ebooks do not need one, and the free number is usable only via KDP. A $99 KDP universal ISBN is reported in a 2024 source. — [TCK Publishing](https://www.tckpublishing.com/how-to-get-an-isbn-number/)
- KDP requires telling Amazon about AI-generated text, images or translations (not AI-assisted work). The answer is private and there is no public badge. Dated Sept-Oct 2023. — [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazons-new-disclosure-policy-for-ai-generated-book-content-is-a-welcome-first-step/); [ADT Magazine](https://adtmag.com/Articles/2023/10/02/AWS-Requires-KDP-authors-to-disclose-AI.aspx)
- Amazon capped daily KDP uploads in 2023. A guide's claim of 3 titles per day is unverified. — [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/); [Scribecount](https://scribecount.com/author-resource/protecting-books/kdp-ai-content-policy-the-compliance-guide)

### Gaps
- Amazon's own KDP content guidelines were not read. Copyright registrability of AI output (US Copyright Office) and per-tool output ownership terms were not researched.

## Workflow, budget and human review

### Takeaway
A rough budget can be assembled from the figures above, but it rests on unverified third-party prices.

### Inferences
- Illustrative lean setup, one-time plus first-year: one $20/month chatbot ($240), Reedsy Studio (free), free ISBN from KDP, a Midjourney or Firefly cover ($60-120), free grammar tier. Approximately $300-$450 for one title, with no human editor.
- Illustrative comfortable setup: Sudowrite or NovelCrafter ($100-700/yr), Atticus $147 or Vellum about $200-250, Publisher Rocket $199 or KDSpy $79, a human cover (about $880 average), a copy edit (about $2,160 for 80k words), Bowker ISBN $125-295. Approximately $3,500-$5,500 for the first title. These are my arithmetic on reported figures, not sourced totals.
- Human review is essential at: story-bible continuity, factual checking of nonfiction, cover and image rights, AI disclosure answers, and audio publishing choices.

### Gaps
- No source describing an end-to-end KDP AI workflow was read in full. Kindlepreneur, Creative Penn and Jane Friedman were not accessed.
